Description

Enjoy efficient, gentle and powerful cleaning even at low temperatures with the Samsung 9kg EcoBubble™ Front Loader Washing Machine (WW90TA046AX). Featuring Samsung's ground-breaking EcoBubble™ technology, Hygiene Steam sanitizing cycle, whisper-quiet Digital Inverter motor with an industry-leading 20-year warranty, and a spacious 9kg Diamond Drum that effortlessly handles bulky king-size duvets.

Key Features & Performance Highlights:

  • EcoBubble™ Technology: Transforms detergent into rich penetrating bubbles before the wash begins, cleaning 40x deeper at cooler temperatures (15°C/20°C) to save up to 70% electricity.
  • Hygiene Steam Sanitizing: Releases deep steam from the bottom of the drum to saturate every garment, eliminating 99.9% of bacteria and household allergens without harsh bleach.
  • Digital Inverter Motor (20-Year Warranty): Brushless magnet motor delivers ultra-quiet 1400 RPM spin performance, minimal vibration and extreme energy efficiency (A+++ rating).
  • Drum Clean+ Cycle: Keeps the interior hygienically fresh by removing 99.9% of odor-causing bacteria from the drum and door gasket without expensive detergents.
  • 15-Minute Quick Wash: Clean lightly soiled clothes in just 15 minutes when you are in a rush.
  • StayClean Detergent Drawer: Specially engineered water flushing system rinses away detergent residue completely, keeping the dispenser spotless.
  • Child Safety Lock & Volt Control: Built-in surge protection guards against South African voltage spikes and power fluctuations.
